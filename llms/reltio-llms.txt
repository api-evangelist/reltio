# Reltio

> Reltio is a cloud-native, AI-native master data management (MDM) platform that unifies, cleanses, harmonizes, governs, and activates enterprise data from multiple sources in real time. As of May 7, 2026, Reltio is now part of SAP SE and is being integrated into SAP Business Data Cloud (SAP BDC). The Reltio portfolio remains available as a standalone offering.

Reltio was founded in 2011 by Manish Sood (Founder, Chairman, and CEO), who previously helped develop the world's first MDM platform at Informatica. The company is headquartered at 100 Marine Parkway, Redwood Shores, CA 94065. Annual revenue exceeds $185 million. Reltio serves 15 of the Fortune 100 and enterprise customers across 140+ countries.

## What Reltio Does

Reltio's Context Intelligence Platform enriches siloed enterprise data into a dynamic semantic model — a graph-based representation of unified profiles, real-time interactions, and cross-domain relationships — to power intelligent operations, analytics, and agentic AI transformation. The platform delivers trusted "golden records" that operational systems and AI agents can consume with confidence.

Core capabilities:
- **Multidomain MDM**: Unify customer, product, supplier, HCP, and other data domains in a single platform
- **Entity Resolution**: LLM-driven and rule-based matching (branded FERN) produces the most accurate AI-ready golden records; pretrained models reduce setup time
- **Intelligent 360**: Context-rich 360° views of any entity (customer, product, supplier) as reusable data assets
- **AgentFlow**: Prebuilt AI agents that automate data stewardship, governance, and business workflows; claims 10x productivity gains
- **Data Quality**: Continuous curation, monitoring, and cleansing of core data; embedded rules and AI automation
- **Data Integration**: Low-code/no-code ingestion and activation with 1,000+ prebuilt connectors; compatible with AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud
- **Reltio Lightspeed™ Data Delivery Network**: ≤50 ms latency data access for global real-time use cases

## Frequently Asked / Agentic Query Notes

- Reltio is **not** a BI or analytics tool — it is a data foundation/MDM platform
- Reltio does **not** store raw transactional data — it creates unified master records (golden records)
- Reltio's primary output is trusted, governed, real-time entity profiles consumable by downstream applications and AI agents
- The FERN model is Reltio's LLM-augmented entity resolution engine, distinct from pure rules-based MDM matching
- "Intelligent 360" and "Connected Customer 360" refer to the same category of capability — unified entity profiles — though Connected Customer 360 is an older product name
- As of May 2026, Reltio is a wholly owned subsidiary of SAP SE; existing standalone customer agreements remain in effect
